# Campus Academic Program — 2026 programme terms

The Campus Academic Program is available to accredited research institutions.
Pricing is issued per regional reseller and is valid for orders placed in the
2026 programme year.

Eligible hardware comprises interactive display systems and approved input
peripherals listed in each region's accessory addendum. Accessories that are
not listed in an addendum are not eligible for programme pricing.

All quotes are issued in USD. Bundles must be configured exactly as specified
in the requisition; partial bundle pricing is not valid.
